E1; decision 1 bears on the CLAUDE.md 4 h rule.

1. **Per-launch time bound: one batch, which cannot go below one evaluation, and one evaluation can exceed 4 h.**
   - A single poll can exceed 4 h: up to 14 new evaluations, or a completion of up to 30, at about 1.4 h each.
   - The launch unit is therefore a **batch within a poll** (`--launch-unit batch`, the default), under the same
     resume rules.
   - One launch is one harness batch: `concurrency` evaluations run in parallel, plus a zero-solve replay and the
     pin checks (seconds; re-hashing about 6 GB of prior eval dirs adds tens of seconds).
   - Its wall time is that of the slowest evaluation in the batch. Measured on the 33 on-disk 2×, production-exit,
     cap-500 records (hashed in the gate JSON, `per_launch_time_bound`):

     | Statistic | Value |
     |---|---|
     | Median | 1.41 h |
     | p90 | 1.68 h |
     | Max | 2.96 h (337 cycles) |
     | Per cycle | 27–34 s |

   - An evaluation that runs to the **cap of 500 cycles** would take about **4.7 h**. No 2× record did, and an
     evaluation cannot be split.
   - Options:
     - (a) accept it: Addendum 85 ruling 9 / D1 covers runs over 4 h within the declared counts;
     - (b) rule that a launch be stopped at 4 h. That would need a harness time limit, which changes the
       configuration.
   - Recommendation: (a). For information: the `poll` unit takes ⌈new / concurrency⌉ × that time, e.g. about 20 h
     for a 14-point poll at concurrency 1. It suits short polls only. E1 should launch by batch.
2. **A commit is needed between launches.** A resume requires the earlier launch records and manifests to be
   git-tracked and clean. This is the cache loader's rule, and the ruling asks for "the same root-scoping".
   - Each launch therefore needs a commit of `e1_launch_<NNN>.json`, its manifest and its launch log before the next
     launch. Eval dirs are hashed, not committed.
   - For E1 at concurrency 1 that is 7 seed launches plus up to 30 per arm.
   - Option: hash-only, i.e. drop the git condition.
   - Recommendation: keep the git condition.
3. **A harness fault now stops the arm at the launch where it occurs.** By a fault I mean a `harness_error` point,
   or a flex / model read-back that does not match (`point_problems`).
   - v2 treated it as a barrier, ran the arm to its end, and exited 1 only then.
   - v3 writes the launch record with the problem and exits 1, and the next resume refuses.
   - This follows CLAUDE.md (a harness fault stops the campaign). Please confirm.
4. **The poll unit means one launch per *evaluating* poll.** A poll that needs no new evaluation is processed by
   whichever launch reaches it. That covers all-cache-hit polls, cap refusals, budget refusals and the terminating
   poll. So a launch boundary falls only where a live evaluation is about to start. Please confirm the reading.
5. **For information: a crashed launch blocks the next resume.** A launch killed mid-batch leaves eval dirs without
   a launch record. The next resume refuses them as foreign files, and nothing is recovered automatically.
   Recovering, for example by moving the partial eval dir aside, would need a ruling at the time.
6. **The dry-run output was renamed to `e1_launcher_v3_dryrun.json`** (schema `…_dryrun_v3`), in the spirit of B+
   choice (e). The five B+ choices of ruling 3 (iv) and the window of 10 (v) are unchanged.

  with `parent_view`. It refuses an existing eval dir, as H.evaluate does.

  same stub object, in the same root path, with a deep-copied cache.
