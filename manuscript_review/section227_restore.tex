\subsubsection{\textcolor{blue}{Recourse Evaluation and Certification}}
\label{subsubsec:certification}

\textcolor{blue}{
    The recourse value of a plan is the expected discounted operating cost of the coupled system over the horizon, given by:
}

\textcolor{blue}{
    \begin{equation}
        Q(\boldsymbol{x}) =
        \sum_{y \in Y} \frac{Y_y}{(1+r)^{y-y_0}}
        \sum_{d \in D} D_d
        \sum_{m \in \Omega_M} \sum_{o \in \Omega_O} \omega_m \, \omega_o \,
        C^{\text{Op}}_{y,d,m,o}(\boldsymbol{x})
        \label{eq:recourse_value}
    \end{equation}
}

\noindent
\textcolor{blue}{
    where $C^{\text{Op}}_{y,d,m,o}$ is the daily operating cost of representative day $d$ of year $y$ under market scenario $m$ and operation scenario $o$, at the consensus point of the TSO--DSO coordination procedure (\ref{app:admm_updated}): the cost of TN generation, of activated DN flexibility and of load curtailment, and the penalties on the storage closure slack and on the network feasibility slacks; in the multi-scenario instance it also includes the commitment charge and the deviation part of the interface settlement (Subsection~\ref{subsubsec:commitment}).
    The contracted part of the settlement is a transfer between operators and is excluded, as are the regularisation terms of the storage agent and of the solvers; renewable curtailment carries no cost and is reported as energy.
    Each representative year stands for a block of $Y_y$ calendar years, and its cost is weighted by $Y_y$ and discounted at the representative year.
}
