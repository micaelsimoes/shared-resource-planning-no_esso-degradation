# Supplementary material — "Degradation-Aware Planning of Shared Battery Energy Storage Systems for Coordinated Transmission and Distribution System Operation"

Simões, Peças Lopes, Soares — Journal of Energy Storage, revised version. The version number and content hash of this archive are given in the generated section below and in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

This archive holds the data behind the manuscript's case study and the complete record behind its reported
comparisons. Every file is generated by a script (`manuscript_review/gen_supplement.py` in the project repository)
from the archived case files and from the frozen result tables that also produced the manuscript's tables; nothing in
it is typed by hand, and a verifier checks every value against those sources (0 mismatches over the 1,623 entries of
the evaluation record and the 70,851 case-file cells at generation). `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the hash of every file.

## Conventions (identical to the manuscript)

- Q is the gross operational cost over the 15-year horizon, discounted at 2 % to 2025, with the contracted component
  of the interface settlement excluded as a transfer between operators; the net form subtracts the terminal salvage
  credit of Appendix B.1; the value of a plan is Q(0) − Q(x) at the same flexibility price; F = I + Q.
- τ = 4,539.07 € is the objective tolerance of the acceptance rule (δ_R = 0.07 on the reference value
  259,375.33 €). A difference between two certified evaluations is determinate when |Δ| ≥ max{3b, 2τ}, with b the
  larger window range; with an uncertified evaluation it must exceed three times the largest consensus gap or settling
  slack involved, in gross and gap-corrected terms (manuscript §2.2.7 and Appendix A.5).
- Amounts in the Appendix-E files are in k€ as printed in the manuscript (rounded to 0.1 k€; ratios to two decimals);
  the case-file data are at the full precision of the files.
- Plans are written node: converter rating (MVA) / energy capacity (MWh); investment year 2025 unless stated.
- Empty cells mean not applicable or not recorded; booleans are yes/no.

The remainder of this file is generated: the acceptance-rule parameters as read from the code, and the column
definitions of every file.
