### CONFIRM — which market and operation scenario the single-scenario instance carries

**Production's scenario selection.** All lines are at repo HEAD `dbe92713`, and the working tree equals HEAD for every
cited file.

- **Market** (`shared_resources_planning.py`, blob `050bc411…`):
  - number of scenarios: `planning_problem.num_market_scenarios = planning_data['NumMarketScenarios']` (l. 8877);
  - seed: `market_seed = derive_random_seed(planning_problem.random_seed, 'market')` (l. 9112);
  - pool: a 100-profile synthetic pool per season (`_generate_market_price_scenarios`, l. 9113–9116; seeded only by
    `market_seed`);
  - draw, per (year, day): `synthetic_profiles['energy'][day].sample(n=NumMarketScenarios,
    random_state=derive_random_seed(market_seed, 'selection', 'energy', year, str(day)))` (l. 9135–9138); the same
    for flexibility;
  - probabilities 1/n (l. 9126).
- **Operation** (`network.py`, blob `ffa61728…`; `network_data.py`, blob `12b239e0…`):
  - pool: per network, a 100-profile pool seeded by `derive_random_seed(network_planning.random_seed,
    'synthetic_profiles')` (`network_data.py:167`);
  - block seed: `derive_random_seed(network_planning.random_seed, 'realization', int(year), str(day))`
    (`network_data.py:190`);
  - draw: **every load** (pc, qc, pc_flex, flex up, flex down) and **every RES generator** (pg, qg) draws its own
    rows, `sample(n=num_operation_scenarios, random_state=derive_random_seed(network.random_seed, 'load', id, …))`
    (`network.py:1427`, `1469`). An "operation scenario s" is therefore the s-th draw of each element, not a joint
    draw;
  - network seeds: `shared_resources_planning.py:8906` (DSO: `'network', 'dso', name, node`) and `:8950` (TSO);
  - `derive_random_seed` is a sha256 of the labels (`helper_functions.py:37`, blob `8c23f978…`);
  - growth uses `initial_year = years[0]` (`network_data.py:160`), which is 2025 in both instances.
- **No seed depends on the scenario count or on the year list**, only on the year value. `DataFrame.sample(n)` with an
  integer seed takes a prefix of one permutation. So **the single-scenario instance carries, in every (year, day)
  block, the first row of production's draw**, for market and operation alike. Any instance derived from the same
  case with more scenarios holds that row as its scenario 1, in every year the two share.
